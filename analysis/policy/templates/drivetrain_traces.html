Diff Detent Traces

A policy standing on a zero command. Roll, steer (its target dashed) and yaw (heading, about the run's median) share each column's time axis. The roller angle is wrapped to one roller revolution; the black line is where the rollers would be with no slop (k_roller × ring-vs-hub). Solid grey lines are the detent line, one per notch (7.5° of ring); dashed grey lines are midway. What the solid line means depends on the detent: a friction bump is where the shaft sticks, a sawtooth flip pushes it away.
